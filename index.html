What Inaza Is & Is Not

✓ What it IS

  • Auto-checking repetitive discount & ID documents
  • Reading uploads and comparing to the policy
  • Updating tasks & writing notes in Modotech
  • Flagging exceptions with the exact reason

✗ What it is NOT

  • Making underwriting decisions for you
  • Replacing your judgment or role
  • Changing eligibility, rating, or pricing
  • Touching anything outside these specific checks
  • A black box — every result is explainable
  • Clearing completed tasks off your queue

Troubleshooting Common Issues

When a task doesn't behave as expected, here's how to diagnose it.

Issue Why It Happens What To Do
Blurry or unreadable document The Inaza OCR engine can't read a poor-quality scan or image. This is common with faxes or low-res uploads. Take the appropriate underwriting action. Request a better-quality scan from the customer if needed, then clear the task.
Wrong document type The document uploaded doesn't match the check. Example: uploading a renewal declaration for a prior coverage validation. Check uploaded documents and compare the upload date with the task creation date. If the doc was uploaded by mistake, clear the task. If a new doc needs review, request it.
Name or address mismatch Some affiliated agencies use different legal names or addresses than what's in our master file. The system can't reconcile the mismatch. Manually verify the document against the policy and agency records. Use your judgment before overriding the system.
A few days short on coverage The system is exact on date calculations. 179 days ≠ 180 days. It fails instead of approximating. Apply your underwriting criteria and judgment on borderline day counts. The system enforces the rule strictly; you apply professional judgment.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about the UW Review Doc system and workflows.

About Inaza

What is Inaza? >
Inaza is an AI-powered document validation platform that we use to automate underwriting document review. It reads policy documents, property records, and ID documents, then applies business rules to validate discounts and requirements. Inaza handles the routine validation work while our underwriters focus on exceptions and complex cases.
How does Inaza work? >
When an agent uploads a document in Modotech, Inaza automatically: (1) reads the document using OCR technology, (2) extracts key data like dates, names, and addresses, (3) applies our business rules to validate against policy requirements, (4) writes a PASS or FAIL result back to the Modotech task, and (5) flags exceptions for your review. The entire process happens in minutes.
Will Inaza touch documents? >
No. Inaza reads documents through OCR (optical character recognition) — the same technology that reads a scanned check. It extracts text and data but never modifies or stores original documents. All documents remain in Modotech exactly as uploaded by the agent.

System & Workflow

What happens if the system fails on a document? >
If Inaza can't read a document (poor quality, wrong format), the task will fail and appear in your queue. You review and take the appropriate underwriting action. See the troubleshooting section in the handbook for guidance.
Can I override a PASS result? >
Yes. The system provides automation, but your underwriting judgment takes priority. If you disagree with any result, document your decision and proceed accordingly.
How long does processing take? >
Most documents are processed within minutes of upload. Complex cases may take longer depending on document quality and volume.

Troubleshooting

Date discrepancies — which format is correct? >
US/Modotech: 5/27/2026 (M/DD/YYYY)
INAZA notes: 2026-05-27 (YYYY-MM-DD)
Mexico/LatAm: 27/05/26 (DD/MM/YY)

When dates don't match, verify the source document directly.
What if an address doesn't match exactly? >
The system uses smart matching (Rd = Road, St = Street). If it still fails, manually verify the document against the policy. Some agencies use different legal addresses.
The system passed something I think should fail — what do I do? >
Document your concern and retask to the INAZA Queue with a note explaining why. The team audits these cases regularly.

Reporting & Escalation

When should I retask to INAZA Queue? >
Only when you believe the system made an error. Before escalating, verify: (1) you're using the correct document, (2) date formats are interpreted correctly, (3) the policy data matches what the system reviewed.
How long does INAZA take to respond to an issue? >
Standard SLA is 24 hours on business days. Urgent issues can be flagged in your retask note.
